Company Overview

Who We Are

Plus Alliance Meridian Ltd is an international trading and industrial development company providing services in mining, renewable energy, infrastructure development, commodities trading, and procurement. Headquartered in Accra, Ghana, the company operates across multiple international markets and connects global industries with strategic resources.

Our Journey

Our History

From humble beginnings to a diversified international enterprise.

2021

Founded in China

Originally established as Hubei, focusing on energy solutions and general goods supply.

2022

African Expansion

Expanded operations across Africa, establishing key trade routes and partnerships.

2023

PAM Established

Plus Alliance Meridian Ltd was established as the parent company overseeing growing operations.

Present

Global Enterprise

A diversified international enterprise operating in mining, construction, renewable energy, and global trade.

Our Vision

To become a leading global player in international trade, connecting diverse markets and providing seamless access to a wide range of products while maintaining high ethical standards, customer focus, and innovative practices to consistently deliver value to all stakeholders; essentially, aiming to be a trusted and efficient facilitator of global commerce with a strong reputation for reliability and quality.

Our Mission

Engage in trade with focus on quality services to customers and professional competences. Develop new markets and products and expand business in Industrial Raw Materials, Commodities and Engineering Equipment and Projects. To Serve as effective instrument of public policy and social responsibility.

Our Philosophy

Our focus is creating a culture of inclusiveness and belonging where all are welcome. Exceeding customer expectations in everything we do and holding ourselves accountable at every step. Embracing change and innovation and looking for new ways to grow our organization and ourselves. Providing exceptional results in the shortest amount of time possible, embracing trends and sustainability, practices and changing the world.

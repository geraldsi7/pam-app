  ## Premium West African Cocoa Beans

  PAM offers premium cocoa beans from sustainable farming cooperatives across West Africa. Our cocoa products are renowned for their exceptional flavor profile and consistent quality.

  ### Product Specifications

  - **Type**: Forastero and Trinitario varieties
  - **Grade**: Premium fermented beans
  - **Moisture Content**: 6-8%
  - **Bean Count**: 80-120 beans per 100g
  - **Fermentation**: 5-7 days traditional fermentation

  ### Quality Standards

  - **Certification**: UTZ, Rainforest Alliance, and Fair Trade certified
  - **Origin**: Traceable to specific farming cooperatives
  - **Taste Profile**: Well-balanced flavor with fruity and chocolate notes
  - **Defect Level**: <3% defective beans

  ### Processing & Packaging

  - **Drying**: Sun-dried to optimal moisture levels
  - **Storage**: Climate-controlled warehouses
  - **Packaging**: Hessian bags (64kg) or bulk containers
  - **Shelf Life**: 18-24 months under proper storage conditions

  ### Applications

  - Chocolate and confectionery manufacturing
  - Cocoa powder and cocoa butter production
  - Beverage industry (drinking chocolate)
  - Cosmetic and pharmaceutical applications

  ### Sustainability

  - Farmer cooperative partnerships
  - Sustainable farming practices
  - Community development programs
  - Environmental conservation initiatives

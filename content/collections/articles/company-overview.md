Plus Alliance Meridian Ltd (PAM) is a Ghana-based limited liability company established on March 19, 2024, with a registered capital of US$200,000 and headquarters in Accra, Ghana.

PAM operates as a multi-sector solutions provider across minerals and mining, commodities and general goods supply, construction and real estate, renewable energy, and procurement and trade services.

We support local and international markets with reliable supply chains, engineering services, and infrastructure delivery while expanding strategic partnerships across Africa, China, and other global markets.

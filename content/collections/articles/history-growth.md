PAM began in 2021 in China under the name Hubei, initially focused on energy and general goods supply before expanding into solar and renewable projects.

As operations matured, Plus Alliance Meridian Ltd was established in Ghana as the parent company, broadening the portfolio to include mining, commodities, construction, consultancy, and international procurement.

The company has grown from 3 employees to more than 20 direct and 100+ indirect staff, with active operations and partnerships in Ghana, Nigeria, China, East Africa, the USA, and the Netherlands.

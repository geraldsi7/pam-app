The 2026 Africa-China Business Investment Summit is proudly organized by trusted partners and positioned around the AfCFTA era, the summit connects African industrialists, Chinese manufacturers and government leaders through direct deal-making and strategic collaboration. These are distinguished partners whose collective experience bridges public policy, investment strategy, and global engagement. Together, they guide ACBIS’s mission to connect capital with opportunity and shape the future of investment in Liberia.

## Strategic Partnership for Clean Energy Future

Plus Alliance Meridian Ltd today announced a landmark partnership with Europe's leading lithium-ion battery manufacturer to supply high-grade lithium carbonate for electric vehicle and renewable energy storage applications.

### Partnership Details

The five-year agreement positions PAM as a key supplier of battery-grade lithium carbonate, with initial annual volumes of 10,000 metric tons scaling to 50,000 metric tons by year three.

"This partnership represents a significant milestone in West Africa's contribution to the global clean energy transition," said Dr. Emmanuel Kofi Mensah, PAM CEO. "We're committed to responsible mineral development that creates sustainable value for our communities and supports global decarbonization efforts."

### Economic Impact

The agreement is expected to generate $2.5 billion in revenue over the contract period while creating 2,500 direct jobs and supporting numerous indirect employment opportunities across the mining and logistics sectors.

### Sustainable Development Focus

PAM's lithium operations incorporate industry-leading environmental standards, including:

- Zero-waste water management systems
- Renewable energy-powered processing facilities
- Community benefit sharing programs
- Biodiversity conservation initiatives

### Market Context

With global demand for lithium projected to increase tenfold by 2030, this partnership ensures West Africa's strategic minerals contribute to the world's clean energy future while establishing Ghana as a leading supplier of critical battery materials.
